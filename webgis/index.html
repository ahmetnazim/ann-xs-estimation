Unclamped Reach Slopes (S > 10−5) M4 (1.321 m) & M5 (1.333 m) Lead

On the 79.1% of CONUS reaches without NHDPlus S = 10−5 slope clamping, physics-informed M4 & M5 outperform M2-XGB (1.400 m; Δ = −0.068 m) and M1 (1.572 m).
